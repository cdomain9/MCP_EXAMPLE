# Financial_Sample.xlsx Context

This file contains sales and financial data with the following columns:

- **Segment**: Business segment (e.g., Government, Midmarket)
- **Country**: Country of sale
- **Product**: Product name
- **Discount Band**: Discount category
- **Units Sold**: Number of units sold
- **Manufacturing Price**: Cost to manufacture
- **Sale Price**: Price sold
- **Gross Sales**: Total sales before discounts
- **Discounts**: Discount amount
- **Sales**: Net sales after discounts
- **COGS**: Cost of goods sold
- **Profit**: Net profit
- **Date**: Transaction date
- **Month Number**: Month as a number
- **Month Name**: Month as a name
- **Year**: Year of transaction

## Example KPIs
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Average Discount
- Units Sold by Product
- Sales by Country
- Profit Margin
- Top Performing Segment
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Yearly Profit
- COGS Analysis

## Related Files
- Financial_Sample.xlsx: Raw data
